# Changelog

The update window shows the section of the version it offers.

## 0.2.0

- A new panel: visitors with the change against the previous period, people online now, a chart you can hover, pageviews, visits, bounce rate and the top pages.
- The menu bar shows how many people are on your website now. Choose what it shows in Settings.
- The API key is checked before it is saved, and a revoked key asks for a new one.
- English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, German and French, following the system or chosen in Settings.
- Open at login, and updates from inside the app.
