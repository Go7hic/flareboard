# Flareboard Features

Flareboard combines core website analytics with PostHog-like product analytics and Cloudflare-native infrastructure.

## Privacy and Trust

- Privacy-first analytics.
- Cookieless tracking support.
- Data ownership through Cloudflare-native deployment.
- No fingerprinting by default.

## Platform

- Edge ingest on Cloudflare Workers.
- Cloudflare stack: Workers, D1, R2, KV, Queues, and Durable Objects.
- Noncommercial self-hosting.

## Analytics

- Website stats, realtime views, segments, custom events, and CSV export.
- People, groups, stickiness, and period comparisons.

## Reports

- Funnels, retention, attribution, UTM reporting, breakdowns, web vitals, goals, cohorts, and journeys.

## Sessions and Experience

- Session replay and heatmaps on the Cloud plan.
- Session timelines and declarative event tracking.

## Experimentation and Feedback

- Feature flags and experiments on the Cloud plan.
- Surveys and feedback analysis on the Cloud plan.

## Quality and Observability

- Error tracking.
- Logs and traces.
- AI observability for provider/model usage, token cost, latency, errors, and quality signals.

## Automation and Warehouse

- Actions, annotations, workflows, and D1 warehouse workflows.
- Warehouse SQL, saved queries, history, scheduled query metadata, and data sources are Cloud plan capabilities.

## Collaboration and Operations

- Teams on the Cloud plan.
- Share links, boards, links, pixels, email reports, CSV import, revenue tracking, admin console, OAuth sign-in, and commercial licensing.
